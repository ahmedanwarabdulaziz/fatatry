# Elfatatry: Google Ads Launch Plan (Test Phase, 3–4 Weeks)

**Goal:** win new non-Arab customers (Canadian, Pakistani, Indian and other families, plus local offices) within ~10 km of Streetsville.
**Campaigns:** 1) Halal Egyptian → `/halal` · 2) Office Catering → `/office-catering`
**All ad text below is checked against Google's character limits** (headlines ≤ 30, descriptions ≤ 90, sitelinks ≤ 25, callouts ≤ 25).

---

## 0. Before launch (must do)

| # | Task | Why |
|---|------|-----|
| 1 | Send Claude the Google Ads conversion ID (`AW-…`) | So calls and direction clicks on the landing pages count as conversions |
| 2 | Link Google Business Profile to Google Ads | Location asset (address, rating, map) appears under your ads |
| 3 | Brief staff on the offer: guest shows `/halal` page → free hot drink after the meal | The offer only works if every server knows it |
| 4 | Put a simple tally sheet at the counter: date + "hot drink offer" | This is how you count real visits from the ads |
| 5 | Make sure the website is deployed and `/halal` and `/office-catering` open on a phone | Ads go to these pages |

## 1. Shared settings (both campaigns)

| Setting | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Campaign type | **Search** only. Untick "Display Network" and "Search Partners" |
| Location | **Radius 10 km** around *117 Queen St S, Mississauga, ON L5M 1K7* |

## 6. What to measure

| Metric | Healthy range to start | Where |
|--------|------------------------|-------|
| Click-through rate (CTR) | 5%+ | Google Ads |
| Avg. cost per click | $1–3 | Google Ads |
| Calls from ads | trending up week over week | Google Ads (call asset) |
| Direction clicks / calls on landing page | — | Google Ads conversions (after tracking is added) |
| **Hot drink redemptions** | the real number of new guests | Counter tally sheet |
| Catering enquiries by phone | 2+ per month to start | Staff log: "How did you hear about us?" |

**Week 3–4 decision:** compare **cost per call** and **cost per redemption** between the two campaigns. Move budget to the winner, then add the next segment: `/vegetarian` or `/try-egyptian` (Claude can build each page in minutes from the same template).

## 7. Later improvements

- Add a **Performance Max** campaign only after 30+ conversions per month, not before.
